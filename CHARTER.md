# Charter

## Purpose

The Transaction Processing Network Interoperability Laboratory (TPNIL) advances reliable interoperability on the BSV Blockchain through impartial evaluation, reproducible testing and durable evidence. Participation is open on equal published terms to individuals, commercial entities, proprietary implementations and open-source projects.

## Work

TPNIL maintains Interoperability and Standardization Initiatives (ISIs), the Integrated Testing and Conformance Validation Environments (ITCVEs) in which software is assessed, and the tooling, vectors and evidence needed to reproduce findings. Each System Under Test (SUT) has an exact artifact identity and configuration. Findings identify the requirements, environment, versions, platforms and evidence actually evaluated.

## Principles

- Publish criteria before scored evaluation and apply them equally to all implementations.
- Use independent expected outcomes; reference behavior alone does not establish correctness.
- Report successful, failed, blocked and inconclusive attempts, with coverage and limitations.
- Preserve raw evidence, exact artifact identities and result corrections.
- Distinguish byte-exact replay from semantic repeatability and disclose reproduction limits.
- Keep sponsorship and affiliations separate from technical judgment.
- Use isolated identities and the selected profile's declared funding and network boundary.

P2PPSR operates the laboratory under [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Original project materials use [Apache-2.0](LICENSE.md); third-party materials retain their own licenses. The BSV focus defines the laboratory's research priorities and does not restrict reuse of its Apache-licensed materials.

Proprietary SUTs are welcome under the [submission terms](legal/ARTIFACT-SUBMISSION-AGREEMENT.md), without source disclosure or transfer of ownership. Those terms cover participation announcements and continued evaluation and hosting of identified submissions.

Outputs remain experimental as recorded in [implementation status](docs/implementation-status.md). Conformance findings address only the evaluated behavior. Certification requires a separately established program. The repository remains private until P2PPSR authorizes release.
