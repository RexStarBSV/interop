# Governance

## Stewardship

PEER TO PEER PRIVACY SYSTEMS RESEARCH LLC (P2PPSR), an Oregon limited liability company, operates TPNIL and appoints its maintainers. Ty Everett is the initial maintainer. Maintainers manage technical requirements, contributions, releases, evaluation infrastructure and result corrections. Responsibilities grow through sustained contribution and sound judgment.

The repository remains private until P2PPSR authorizes public release. TPNIL is independent of BSV Association. Participation does not establish organizational affiliation or authority to represent another party.

## Technical decisions

Anyone may propose improvements or challenge an interpretation through an issue or pull request. Proposals identify the affected requirements, rationale, compatibility impact and evidence. Maintainers seek rough consensus and explain substantive decisions in the repository. Requirements and oracles are fixed before evaluations that rely on them; changes follow [versioning](docs/versioning.md).

Sponsorship, payment, affiliation and contribution history confer no preference in evaluation. Reviewers disclose relevant conflicts and recuse themselves from deciding their own disputed results. The same technical requirements apply to reference implementations and other submissions.

## Changes, disputes and appeals

A result challenge identifies the artifact, requirement and supporting evidence. Maintainers investigate and record the resolution. Where available, an unconflicted reviewer outside the original decision reviews the dispute. Unresolved disputes remain visible. Corrections reference the original evidence; revised software receives a new artifact identity and evaluation.

Current outputs are experimental. Participation announcements and test results do not imply endorsement or certification. A future certification program requires separately published criteria and independent review before any certification claim is issued.

## Custody and continuity

Maintainers preserve evaluation evidence, third-party notices, and accepted submission permissions. Changes of operator preserve existing grants and the meaning of retained results. P2PPSR authorizes changes of stewardship, licensing policy and public access; no contributor membership, council accession or separate governance agreement is required to use or contribute to the project.
