# Laboratory direction

TPNIL operates under P2PPSR stewardship with maintainer-led technical decisions and equal evaluation criteria, as described in [governance](../../GOVERNANCE.md).

Original project materials use [Apache-2.0](../../LICENSE.md). Imported materials retain their own licenses. Proprietary software can participate under the [submission terms](../../legal/ARTIFACT-SUBMISSION-AGREEMENT.md), without transferring ownership or disclosing source.

The repository is private until P2PPSR authorizes release. Current outputs are experimental. The [decision register](../../docs/decisions.md), [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) and [implementation status](../../docs/implementation-status.md) describe technical priorities and execution evidence.
